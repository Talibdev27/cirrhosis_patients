# Cirrhosis Survival Prediction

Predicting the outcome of cirrhosis patients from clinical measurements, as a multiclass probability task. The project is a portfolio piece: clear, defensible reasoning matters more than leaderboard rank.

## Outcome

**Status**:
The outcome recorded for a patient at the end of their Follow-up. One of C, CL or D.
_Avoid_: label, target class, outcome class

**N_Days**:
The length of a patient's Follow-up in days; the moment at which Status is observed.
_Avoid_: duration, time, days

**Follow-up**:
The period from a patient's registration until their Status was recorded.

**C (Censored)**:
The patient was alive at N_Days, without a liver transplant.
_Avoid_: alive, survived

**CL (Censored, Liver transplant)**:
The patient was alive at N_Days because they had received a liver transplant.
_Avoid_: transplanted, alive-with-transplant

**D (Death)**:
The patient had died by N_Days.
_Avoid_: deceased, dead

**Excluded label**:
A Status value present in the training data that is not one of C, CL or D (seen: `Y`). Treated as noise and out of scope.
_Avoid_: fourth class, unknown status

## Data

**Competition data**:
The train and test sets for this competition: synthetic rows generated by a deep-learning model trained on the Original dataset, with similar but not identical feature distributions.
_Avoid_: real data, patient records

**Original dataset**:
The real Mayo Clinic primary biliary cirrhosis study data (the "Cirrhosis Patient Survival Prediction" dataset) from which the Competition data was generated.
_Avoid_: source data, external data

## Clinical features

**Mayo risk score**:
The published Mayo Clinic survival risk score for primary biliary cirrhosis, combining age, bilirubin, albumin, prothrombin time and edema; developed on the same patient population as the Original dataset.
_Avoid_: liver score, risk index

**Edema**:
Fluid retention status: N (none), S (present but resolved, or controlled by diuretics), Y (present despite diuretics).
_Avoid_: swelling

## Evaluation

**Log loss**:
The competition metric: multiclass logarithmic loss over the three Status probabilities, after each row is rescaled to sum to one and clipped to [1e-15, 1 − 1e-15].
_Avoid_: score, accuracy
